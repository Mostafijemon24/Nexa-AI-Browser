arXiv:2609.14122[cs.NI]·4 Sep 2026

Segmented Transfer Scheduling for High-Throughput Multi-Source Downloads

R. Aldana, M. Öström, J. Whitfield, P. Nakamura
Distributed Systems Lab · Institute for Open Network Research

Abstract

We present a scheduling framework that partitions a single logical transfer across multiple concurrent connections and, where available, multiple origin mirrors. Unlike naive parallel chunking, our scheduler continuously reallocates byte ranges toward the fastest live segments while respecting per-host connection limits and server-advertised range support. On a corpus of 4,200 real-world files we observe throughput gains that track the gap between a client's link capacity and single-stream server behavior, with negligible benefit when a server already saturates the link.

We further describe a resumable, checksum-verified pipeline suitable for direct browser-to-cloud delivery, and report on failure recovery under connection loss.
